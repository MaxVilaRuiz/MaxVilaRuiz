## About Me

I enjoy building technology and turning ideas into real, meaningful products. My experience spans across the development of web applications, full-stack systems, backend services, and cross-platform solutions, working on different types of architectures and product environments. This background has given me a strong understanding of how systems are designed, built, and scaled in practice.

I’m particularly interested in how technology is built as a team effort, focusing on structured workflows, collaborative practices, and strong foundations in architecture, scalability, and maintainability, while connecting technical execution with product and strategic thinking to ensure what is built is both functional and aligned with real needs.

I enjoy working on projects that sit at the intersection of engineering, product, and impact, especially in environments where ideas are taken from concept to reality through effective teamwork.
